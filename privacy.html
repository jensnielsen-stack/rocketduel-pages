Privacy Policy

Rocket Duel

Last updated October 3, 2026

The short version: Your coins, unlocks, stats and settings stay on your device. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics of our own.

Three things do leave your device: match data sent straight to your opponent's device when you play nearby, your wins sent to Apple's Game Center if you're signed in, and the data Google's ad service collects to show ads. All three are covered in full below.

Deleting the app deletes everything in the first three rows. There is no copy anywhere else.

Play Nearby

PLAY NEARBY connects two devices in the same room directly, over local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, using Apple's own peer-to-peer networking. That's why iOS asks for permission to use your local network and Bluetooth the first time you play nearby. During a match, ship positions, controls and hits are sent straight to the other device and nowhere else. Nothing goes through a server, and nothing is kept after the match ends.

Your device's name is shown to the other player so they can pick you from the list.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your total number of online wins goes to Apple's leaderboard, together with your Game Center player identity, so other players can see it. We never receive this data. Apple handles it under Apple's own privacy policy. Game Center is optional. The game works fully without it.

Advertising

Rocket Duel is free and shows ads through Google's AdMob service:

  • a small banner at the bottom of the main menu and the result screen, never during a match
  • an occasional full-screen ad when you leave the result screen of a one-device match: not before your third match, and never more than once every three minutes. Never in a Play Nearby match, and never between the final hit and the result.
  • only if you choose it, a short video that doubles the coins from the match you just played

Don't want ads at all? A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns every one of them off for good. After that, Google's ad SDK isn't started and none of the data below is collected. The purchase itself is handled entirely by Apple. We only learn from the App Store that this device's Apple Account owns it.

Showing ads means Google's SDK collects some data. This is the exact list, taken from the privacy declarations that Google's SDKs ship with, not from a general description:

The advertising identifier, the ads you've seen and your approximate location are all treated as used to track you across other companies' apps, because Google's ad service can combine ad-measurement data across the apps that use it, even where we don't ask it to. That's why iOS asks your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before it can be used. Ads still show if you say no. Rocket Duel always requests non-personalized ads, whatever you answer.

If you're in the EU, UK or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice the first time it's needed, before any of this happens. You can change that choice at any time from AD PRIVACY in the top-left corner of the main menu.

Children's privacy

Rocket Duel is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are limited to a general-audience content rating.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what data it handles, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
