Rocket Duel

Rocket Duel is a 1v1 rocket dogfight in a cave. You fight against the computer, or against a friend on a second device in the same room. This page covers how to play, the common questions, and how to reach us.

How to play

  • Objective: each ship has 3 lives. Land 3 hits on your opponent to win.
  • Steering: the stick on the left aims your ship. THRUST fires the engine.
  • Firing: FIRE shoots the loaded round. The buttons above it pick the weapon.
  • Refuel and rearm: land gently on your own platform to refill fuel and ammo, and to change weapons.
  • Hazards: cave walls and rocks are harmless, you just bounce off. Crashing into the other ship destroys you both.

The same rules are always available in the app under HELP on the main menu.

Game modes

  • PLAY NEARBY: two devices in the same room, connected directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. No internet needed.
  • PRACTICE (ONE DEVICE): you against the computer. Pick how hard it plays in SETTINGS.
  • AI VS AI: sit back and watch two computer pilots fight it out.

Coins, weapons and skins

Every match you play against someone pays coins: more for a win, a few for a loss. New players start with 300 coins, enough for their first weapon. Spend coins in the SHOP on the Shotgun, Heat-Seeker and Grenade, and on new ship colours. The standard round is always free.

After a match you can choose to watch a short video to double the coins from that match. It's entirely optional.

Ads

Rocket Duel is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. There's a small banner on the main menu and the result screen. There's also an occasional full-screen ad when you leave a one-device match: not before your third match, and at most once every three minutes. There are no ads during a match, and none in Play Nearby matches. See the privacy policy for exactly what data the ad service collects. In the EU, UK or Switzerland, tap AD PRIVACY on the main menu to change your consent choice.

Rather not see ads at all? Tap REMOVE ADS in the top-left corner of the main menu for a one-time purchase. There's no subscription, and it removes every ad, including the optional coin videos.

Frequently asked questions

I tap a weapon and nothing happens.
That weapon isn't unlocked yet. The game shows its price for a moment. Buy it in the SHOP. You can also only switch weapons while landed on your own platform.

PLAY NEARBY doesn't find the other device.
Both devices need Wi-Fi or Bluetooth switched on, and both must have allowed Rocket Duel to use the local network. Check that under Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. Both devices also need the same version of the game from the App Store.

My coins or unlocks disappeared.
They are stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes them. They don't carry over between devices.

I bought Remove Ads, but I'm seeing ads again.
This can happen after reinstalling or on a new device. Tap REMOVE ADS on the main menu, then Restore Purchases. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account, so you never need to buy it twice.

I don't want to sign in to Game Center.
You don't need to. Game Center only keeps the online win leaderboard. Everything else works without it.

Still stuck?

Email us and we'll get back to you. It helps to say which device you use and what you were doing when the problem happened.
