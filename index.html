For iPhone and iPad

Rocket Duel

A 1v1 rocket dogfight in a cave. Fight gravity, manage your fuel, land on your own platform to rearm, and land three hits before your opponent does.

Play nearby

Two devices in the same room, connected directly. No internet or account needed.

Practice

Fight a computer pilot at six difficulty levels, from gentle to ruthless. The sixth studies how you fly and adapts to you. Every match the computer is in a slightly different mood, so no two fights play out the same.

Shield

Block every shot for one second, then wait five for it to recharge. Time it right, and watch your opponent's shield bar.

Four weapons

Standard rounds, Shotgun, Heat-Seeker and Grenade, each needing a different approach. The Heat-Seeker locks on when it gets close, and flies round rocks to find you.

Replays

Every match is saved on your device. Watch it back with the camera framing the action, or zoom by hand.

Earn and unlock

Every match pays coins. Spend them on weapons and new ship colours.

Free, with light ads

Rocket Duel is free. There are no ads during a match, and a one-time Remove Ads purchase turns them off completely. See support for exactly when ads appear, and the privacy policy for what data they involve.
